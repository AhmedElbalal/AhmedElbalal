## Growth strategy (target: up to 200 additional repositories, quality-gated)
- Original projects: hospitality operations, travel technology, commerce, analytics, automation, accessibility, developer tooling.
- Open-source forks: only useful projects with proper upstream attribution and a specific learning or contribution goal.
- No bulk placeholder repositories, duplicate templates, or misleading authorship claims.

## Quality gate for each original project
Problem statement; functioning code; reproducible setup; sample data without personal information; tests; CI where appropriate; clear README; screenshots or demo; license and attribution.
